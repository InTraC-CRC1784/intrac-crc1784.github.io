## Helmholtz NEFELI Platform

&nbsp;

The [NEFELI platform](https://nefeli.helmholtz-munich.de) at Helmholtz Zentrum München is a FAIR-compliant research data repository for structured publication, long-term archiving, and reuse of research datasets.

&nbsp;

- **Structured Data Collection** — Standardised metadata schemas and controlled vocabularies from the start
- **Harmonisation & Quality Control** — Consistent annotation across projects using community ontologies
- **Integration & Analysis Enablement** — Analysis-ready datasets with cross-species and cross-modality compatibility
- **FAIR Publication with DOI** — Every dataset is citable and findable through persistent identifiers
- **Long-Term Accessibility** — Datasets remain accessible beyond the consortium lifecycle
